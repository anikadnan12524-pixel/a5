Sunday best

Sunday roast chicken with lemon and herbs

Prep 15 minCook 1 h 30 minServes 4–6

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, about 1.6 kg (3½ lb)
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 4 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
  • Small bunch of thyme and rosemary
  • 30 g soft butter
  • 1 kg potatoes, peeled and quartered
  • 3 carrots, in chunks
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Take the chicken out of the fridge 30 minutes before cooking. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
  2. Stuff the cavity with the lemon, garlic and half the herbs. Rub the skin with butter and season well.
  3. Toss the potatoes and carrots in oil and seasoning and arrange around the chicken in a roasting tin.
  4. Roast for about 1 hour 20 minutes, basting once or twice, until the juices run clear and the thickest part of the thigh reaches 74°C (165°F).
  5. Rest the chicken under foil for 15 minutes while the potatoes crisp up, then carve.

From the recipe card: Use the pan juices for gravy: see our cooking classics page.

Slow & cosy

Old-fashioned beef stew

Prep 20 minCook 2 h 30 minServes 6

Ingredients

  • 1 kg stewing beef, in chunks
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 3 carrots, sliced
  • 2 celery sticks, sliced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 litre beef stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 500 g potatoes, in chunks

Method

  1. Toss the beef in seasoned flour and brown in batches in a heavy pot. Set aside.
  2. Soften the onions, carrots and celery in the same pot for 8 minutes.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste, return the beef and add the stock, bay and thyme.
  4. Cover and simmer very gently for 1½ hours, or cook in the oven at 160°C (325°F).
  5. Add the potatoes and cook for 40 minutes more until the beef is tender.

From the recipe card: Stew tastes even better the next day, so it’s perfect for making ahead.

Feel-good

Chicken and vegetable soup

Prep 15 minCook 45 minServes 6

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp butter or oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery sticks, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1.5 litres chicken stock
  • 2 cooked chicken breasts or leftover roast chicken, shredded
  • 100 g small pasta or noodles
  • Handful of parsley, chopped

Method

  1. Melt the butter and soften the onion, carrots and celery for 10 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic and cook for a minute, then pour in the stock and bring to a simmer.
  3. Add the pasta and cook until tender, about 8–10 minutes.
  4. Stir in the chicken and heat through. Season and finish with parsley.

From the recipe card: A great way to use leftovers from the Sunday roast.

Crowd-pleaser

Cheesy baked ziti

Prep 15 minCook 40 minServes 6

Ingredients

  • 400 g ziti or penne
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 700 g tomato passata or crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 250 g ricotta
  • 200 g mozzarella, grated
  • 40 g Parmesan, grated

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 190°C (375°F). Cook the pasta for 2 minutes less than the pack says, then drain.
  2. Soften the onion in the oil, add the garlic, tomatoes and oregano and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Mix the pasta with the sauce and dollops of ricotta.
  4. Spread in a baking dish, top with mozzarella and Parmesan and bake for 20–25 minutes until bubbling and golden.

From the recipe card: Assemble the night before and bake straight from the fridge, adding 10 minutes.

Baking day

Simple farmhouse loaf

Prep 20 minCook 35 minServes 1 loaf

Ingredients

  • 500 g strong white bread flour
  • 7 g fast-action dried yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp soft butter or oil
  • 320 ml warm water

Method

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt and sugar, then add the butter and water and bring together into a dough.
  2. Knead for 10 minutes until smooth and springy. Cover and leave to rise for 1 hour until doubled.
  3. Knock back, shape into a loaf and place in a greased 900 g (2 lb) tin. Rise again for 45 minutes.
  4. Bake at 220°C (425°F) for 30–35 minutes until it sounds hollow when tapped underneath. Cool on a rack.

From the recipe card: Leave it to cool completely before slicing for the best texture.

Something sweet

Grandma-style blueberry pie

Prep 30 minCook 50 minServes 8

Ingredients

  • Pastry for a double-crust 23 cm (9 in) pie
  • 700 g blueberries, fresh or frozen
  • 150 g sugar
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp butter, in small pieces
  • 1 egg, beaten, for glazing

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a pie dish with half the pastry.
  2. Toss the blueberries with sugar, cornflour, lemon juice and cinnamon and tip into the case. Dot with butter.
  3. Cover with the remaining pastry, crimp the edges, cut a few steam slits and brush with egg.
  4. Bake on a tray for 45–50 minutes until golden and bubbling through the slits.
  5. Cool for at least 2 hours so the filling sets before slicing.

From the recipe card: Bake on a preheated tray to keep the bottom crust crisp.
